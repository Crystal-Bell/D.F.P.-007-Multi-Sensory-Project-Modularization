​Objective: Compartmentalize complex, multi-sensory project data (e.g., MeBook texture/navigation, M.A.D. W.E.A.R. layouts) into distinct modules to prevent cognitive overload.
​Trigger: Inception of a new creative project or expansion of an existing framework.
​Procedure:
​Deconstruction: Break the project into its sensory or functional components (e.g., visual layout, tactile material, narrative goal).
​Container Assignment: Assign each component to a specific, labeled "module" in your master system.
​Parallel Execution: Work on modules individually rather than attempting to synthesize the entire project at once.
​Synthesis Protocol: Only merge modules when each individual part has met its functional benchmark.
​Philosophy: Complexity is just a collection of simple modules. By keeping projects modular, you maintain fluidity, allowing you to animate and design without losing the "big picture" of your momentum.# D.F.P.-007-Multi-Sensory-Project-Modularization
